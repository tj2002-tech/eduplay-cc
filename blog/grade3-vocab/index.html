📅 May 3, 2026 ✍️ EduPlay Team ⏱️ 8 min read
📚 This list is organized by "word family" instead of unit order — words on the same theme sit together, so parents and teachers can go through one theme at a time and easily match words to textbook units.
Every word includes phonetics, and you can tap to hear it in the Word Family cards.

PEP English for primary school covers about 600+ core words from grade 3 to grade 6. This page organizes them by family theme, each labeled with its grade, so you can jump straight to what you need.

💡 How to use this list: reading the list is not remembering it. After each grade's families, tap "Practice", listen and repeat in the Word Family cards, then play one Word Match round — these three steps retain 3–5× better than reading alone.

How to get the most from this list

Just reading or copying the list barely helps. Research shows passive reading retains only about 10% after 24 hours. Try this three-step routine:

  • Step 1 (5 min): go through one family in the Word Family cards — tap each word, repeat aloud, and study the relationship map to understand the family links.
  • Step 2 (5 min): immediately play one Word Match or Word Pop round for that family, turning "recognizing" into "responding".
  • Step 3 (5 min, next day): go over the same family again the next day, find which words are still shaky, and focus on those.
