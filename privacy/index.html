🔒 Privacy Policy

EduPlay values the privacy of every child and parent

EduPlay ("the Site") is a completely free, no-sign-up online educational game website for primary school. Many of our users are minors, so privacy comes first. This policy explains how we handle information related to the Site.

What We Collect

  • Anonymous usage data: page views, time on site, device type and traffic sources are collected as aggregate statistics via Baidu Analytics and Google Analytics. None of this contains any personally identifiable information such as name, phone, school or address.
  • Local browser progress: achievements, daily check-ins, wrong-word book and level progress are stored in your own browser (localStorage) — never uploaded to any server, never read by us.
  • Contact information: only when you email hi@eduplay.cc do we receive your address and message, used solely to reply to your inquiry.

Children's Privacy

The Site is designed for primary-school students and their parents and teachers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; all analytics are anonymous aggregates that cannot be linked to an individual. If you are a parent or teacher and find any privacy concern, contact us any time at hi@eduplay.cc and we will act promptly.

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site only uses necessary cookies / local storage to: ① record anonymous visit statistics; ② remember your in-browser game progress. We do not serve identity-based tracking ads.

Third-Party Services

To measure site usage, the Site uses the following third-party analytics services, whose data handling is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Baidu Analytics (hm.baidu.com)
  • Google Analytics (analytics.google.com)

You can opt out of anonymous statistics using your browser's "Do Not Track" setting or the opt-out tools each analytics service provides.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personally identifiable information with third parties. Aggregated anonymous statistics are used only to improve the Site's content and experience.

Contact Us

For any questions about this policy, or to delete voluntarily provided contact information, email: hi@eduplay.cc.
