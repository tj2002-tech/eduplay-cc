How to Play

Flip two cards at a time. Match an English word with its Chinese meaning to clear them and score. The round ends when all pairs are cleared.

Tip: Battle mode uses two boards — 🔴 Red on the left, 🔵 Blue on the right. Both teams flip their own cards at the same time; the first to match everything wins. Use Practice mode for solo review.
