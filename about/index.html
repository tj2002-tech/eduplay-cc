About EduPlay

Make the 10-minute recess more fun and more useful

Who We Are

EduPlay is a completely free, no-sign-up, open-and-play online educational game site for primary school. We are a small group of product and teaching enthusiasts focused on basic education, hoping to make "memorizing words" and "practicing mental math" a little lighter in ways kids genuinely enjoy.

What We Do

  • 5 educational mini-games: Word Match, Word Pop, Word Family, Memory Flip and Math Blitz — covering English and math with vocabulary aligned to PEP grades 3 to 7.
  • Learning blog: proven methods like word-family memory and gamified learning, plus printable resources.
🆓 Completely Free 📱 Phone / Tablet / Desktop 🔊 Tap-to-Hear Pronunciation 🏆 Achievements & Check-ins

Our Philosophy

We believe good learning tools should have zero barriers: no paywalls, no forced sign-ups, no disruptive ads — so kids spend their precious recess time on things that truly pay off. All progress stays on your own device; privacy is in your hands.
